Where We Stand

Summary: For all team members and families. What happened, what it means, what we are deciding, and what is not affected.

Last updated: 2026-08-16

The Short Version

Southern California is not running a competitive Future Edition season. All official events and advancement follow Founders Edition, which uses SPIKE hardware we do not have. Our team registered for Future Edition and built everything around it.

Sunday meetings are paused while the coaches work out the best path forward. This is a pause, not an ending.

What Happened, In Order

  1. FIRST launched BIOGLOW with two editions — Founders on SPIKE, Future on Computer Science & AI
  2. We registered for Future Edition on the official FIRST Dashboard, without restriction
  3. Three families each bought a CS & AI kit (#45522)
  4. The team built the whole field, three of four role tools, and ran the game
  5. We asked the SoCal organiser about events and were told: all events and advancement follow Founders Edition, with a possible off-season Future event if enough teams are interested

The frustrating part is the order of those steps. Registration was open and unqualified before anyone told us the regional situation.

This is not the team's fault, and not the kids' fault. Everyone did exactly what the instructions said.

Important Context — This Is Not Just Us

Before anyone concludes SoCal let us down specifically:

  • VA-DC runs Future Edition as a spring 2027 pilot, explicitly "without advancement to additional levels of competition"
  • Future Edition's first season under FIRST is also its last — the programme becomes LEGO League in 2027-28

Future Edition 2026-27 is a pilot year. As far as we can tell, no Future Edition team is advancing anywhere this season. Our kids are not behind their peers; that path does not currently exist for any Future team.

Details and sources: bioglow season and first lego split.

What Is Not Lost

Worth reading twice, because it is easy to feel like the season evaporated.

Status
The kits Still right. CS & AI is required for LEGO League from 2028-29. See equipment inventory
Everything built Field, Driver, Technician and Operator tools — all still ours
What the kids learned Coding, sensors, motors, building, teamwork, sitting in every role
The farm visit Done, and it produced two real problems from real people
The Innovation Project Completely unaffected — see below
The team Five kids who now know each other and how to work together

The Innovation Project does not depend on any of this

The Bermuda grass and rabbit problems, the farm visit, the expert conversation, the prototype — none of it depends on which edition the robot game uses, or on whether a tournament happens.

That work can be finished and shown to someone regardless: back to the farm, a school science night, a community group. Finishing something and presenting it to a real audience is most of the value, and it is still fully available.

The Three Options

The coaches are weighing these. Nothing is decided.

Option 1 — Switch to SPIKE and compete this season

Move to Founders Edition, which has events and advancement in SoCal.
Gains A real competitive season, judging, and an advancement path
Costs SPIKE hardware nobody owns, plus the Founders challenge set — it is a different game with different mission models
Time Roughly 13 weeks to mid-November, starting over
What carries Teamwork, Core Values, the Innovation Project. The robot work does not — Founders is 15 missions with one robot
Side benefit SPIKE also works with FIRST's new K-8 programme through 2028-29

Option 2 — Skip the competitive season; aim at spring and 2027-28

Keep the CS & AI work, target the possible off-season event, and plan for LEGO League in 2027-28.
Gains Nothing already built or bought is wasted; kits are required for LEGO League from 2028-29
Costs No competitive season this year; the spring event is recreational and not guaranteed
Risk A long gap is hard on nine-year-olds. Momentum and team cohesion are real things to protect
Mitigation Finish the Innovation Project anyway and present it somewhere real

Option 3 — A different competition, such as VEX IQ
Gains Active, established competition with a clear pathway upward and its own world championship
Timing The 2026-27 VEX IQ season window runs 1 Aug 2026 – 28 Feb 2027, so entering now is possible
Locally SoCal has active VEX IQ leagues, including LAUSD leagues, and San Diego events have run in past seasons
Costs New hardware, new software, new community. Annual team registration plus event fees
What carries Everything except the hardware — teamwork, engineering habits, presenting, the project

Where to check: robotevents.com lists events by region. Filter for San Diego to see what actually exists and when.

What To Tell The Kids

Straight, and without making it their problem:

"The grown-ups are sorting out which competition we go to. Nothing you built is wasted, and nothing you learned goes away. We are taking a couple of weeks to make a good decision instead of a fast one."

Two things worth them hearing as well:

  • They did everything right. They built the field, coded three tools, played the game, visited the farm and asked real questions. That is a good season's work already, in four weeks.
  • The farm problems are still ours to solve. Bermuda grass and the rabbits do not care which competition we enter.
