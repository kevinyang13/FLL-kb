12-Week Season Plan

Summary: Three-phase FLL season schedule taking a rookie team from kickoff through tournament day.

Sources: docs/FLL_Rookie_Team_Master_Roadmap.pdf

Last updated: 2026-05-11

Phase 1: Foundation & Mechanical Literacy (Weeks 1–3)

  • Kickoff: Establish Team Contract; review Robot Game Rulebook
  • Analysis: Identify "Low-Hanging Fruit" missions vs. "High-Risk" challenges
  • Build: Construct stable drivetrain; begin Engineering Notebook curation

Key output: working drivetrain, team norms established. (source: FLL_Rookie_Team_Master_Roadmap.pdf)

Phase 2: Engineering Design Process (Weeks 4–7)

  • Coding: Intro to Color and Gyro sensors; begin Innovation Project problem identification
  • Outreach: Parents facilitate 1–2 expert interviews to validate kids' solutions
  • Iteration: Build attachments; test for consistency

Design principle: Reliability > Complexity — see robot design principles. (source: FLL_Rookie_Team_Master_Roadmap.pdf)

Phase 3: Integration & Performance (Weeks 8–12)

  • The Drill: Practice full 2.5-minute match runs; draft 5-minute project presentation
  • Mock Judging: Use official FLL Rubrics; practice answering "Why" and "How"
  • The Event: Tournament day framed as celebration of resilience and discovery

(source: FLL_Rookie_Team_Master_Roadmap.pdf)

Calendar Alignment

Season milestones align with US holidays — see fll rookie roadmap for the holiday alert table. Thanksgiving window = tournament qualifier (mandatory attendance).
