Parent Roles

Two-group model:

Group Responsibility
Tech Dads (Robotics & Engineering) Guide robot platform learning, encourage try-fail-retry mindset, explain coding logic without typing code for kids
Project Moms (Innovation & Operations) Guide Innovation Project research, coach presentation skills, handle snacks/scheduling/org

(source: FLL_Rookie_Team_Charter_Registration_Ready.pdf)

Season Schedule

16 weeks, Aug 9 to Nov 15, 2026. Full dates and milestones: calendar. Week-by-week goals: bot builders training plan.

Team Agreements

For parents: - Text group in advance if child will miss a session (pair/group work is disrupted by absences) - "Embrace the Mess" — step back, let kids figure it out

For kids (Team Spirit): 1. Share the fun and the work — robot/computers belong to the whole team 2. Listen to understand — "Tell me more about that idea" not "That won't work" 3. Respect space and teammates — kind words, talk about the idea not the person 4. Celebrate mistakes — ask "What did we learn?" and try a new way

gracious professionalism is the heart of the program: "how we work together is far more important than winning." (source: FLL_Rookie_Team_Charter_Registration_Ready.pdf)

Registration

Form requires: student legal name, preferred nickname, date of birth, parent/guardian contacts (up to 2). Signed by student and parent. Sent to coaches for official FIRST registration.
