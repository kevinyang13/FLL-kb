What the team already knew

From the 4 Oct coaches' letter (bioglow season): one Futures event planned for January or February 2027, no advancement known, a 24-minute judging session (two five-minute presentations plus Q&A, up to two coaches observing), and roughly 60 SoCal Future teams. The event date and place were not announced. The registration dashboard showed the team as event ready on 31 Oct, with no detail.
