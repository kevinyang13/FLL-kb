Futures Event — La Cañada, 30 January 2027

Summary: The official date, place, cost and registration condition for our Southern California Futures event, plus LEGO's answers to coaches' rules questions. All from the organisers' email and Slack channel, 11–13 November 2026.

Sources: Gmail, "Futures Event is happening January 30!" from Cathy Schulz (FIRST California Southern), 11 Nov 2026; Slack digests from the #all-fll-futures-socal channel, 11–13 Nov 2026 (relayed by email notification)

Last updated: 2026-11-14

The event
When Saturday, 30 January 2027 (source: Cathy Schulz email, 11 Nov)
Where La Cañada High School, 4463 Oak Grove Drive, La Cañada Flintridge, CA 91011
Registration fee $200
Registration Through the team portal link in the organisers' email
Run by FIRST California Southern LEGO League program delivery partners: Cathy Schulz, Sarah Rodriguez, Paul Kass

This replaces the Sunday 31 January that the coach gave on 8 Nov (source: coach, 8 Nov 2026). The two sources disagree; the organisers' email is the official one and the site now uses it. Worth one confirming reply to the organisers.

The catch — it may not run

The event only takes place if more than 12 teams have complete registrations by 23 December 2026. A complete registration is all three of:

  1. The team is registered.
  2. The fee is paid.
  3. Two coaches are in compliance with FIRST and California AB506.

Our own status on each of these is not recorded in the wiki and needs checking.

No San Diego event

On 11–12 Nov, Cathy replied in Slack that the organisers could not find a venue in the San Diego area, so San Diego teams travel to La Cañada. She also offered to attend and help judge if a coach hosts a scrimmage locally (source: Slack, 11–12 Nov). This settles the earlier "two sites, East LA and San Diego" plan from the 3 Nov meeting (coaches meeting 2026 11 03): there is one site.

LEGO's answers to coaches' questions (Slack, 13 Nov)

Relayed by Cathy after LEGO replied to questions the coaches asked.

Question Answer (rulebook reference as given)
Token recycler The Specialist may pick up tokens (keystone species and resources) by hand and drop them into the top hole of the recycler; they slide back into the field. Rulebook p.11, §18 Specialist rules, courtesy rules
Average of matches Final game score is the sum of all scores divided by the number of matches played; at an event a team plays at least three matches. Rulebook p.3, §2 Scoring
Attaching tools to barriers Snapping onto the technician and operator barriers is allowed, because both barriers are inside the player area, as long as the tool stays inside the area (p.9, §16; p.10, §17) and nothing is disassembled or modified on a mission model (p.7, §14). Things that snap on and off easily do not break that rule
Poster format Up to the team: 1 poster, 2 posters or any other way of presenting the project and engineering design work

What this means for us: - The average-of-three-matches answer confirms the strategy on match strategy and the board (robot design board). - The poster answer leaves the robot design board free to be a single board. - The barrier answer is relevant to any tool that attaches at the base. Our own tool-to-barrier use should be checked against the three conditions above before relying on it.
