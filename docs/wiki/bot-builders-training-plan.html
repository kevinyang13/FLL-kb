Team Info

  • Team name: Bot Builders
  • Motto: "Dream it, Build it, Solve it!"
  • Season: FLL 2026-2027 BIOGLOW
  • Main meeting: Every Sunday, 4:30–7:30 PM, in-person at coaches' house
  • Weekday sessions: Online project meetings + optional in-person robot practice as needed
  • Parent role: Snacks, expert interviews, mock audiences, supplies, team support

Key Thresholds & Design Rules

  • 40% rule (Week 8): cut any mission with <40% success rate — not worth keeping in the run
  • Design freeze (Week 13): no new features, structural changes, or mission additions after this point
  • First full timed run (Week 9): 2.5-minute match, all missions chained
  • Backup plan: If Sunday goals not met, weekday online sessions used to stay on track

Important FLL Principle

Kids do the work. Parents and coaches facilitate, guide, arrange resources, and encourage learning — but do not build, code, research, or present for the team.
