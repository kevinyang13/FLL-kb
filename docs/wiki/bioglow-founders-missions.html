BIOGLOW Missions — Founders Edition

Summary: All 15 Founders Edition robot game missions with exact scoring. This is not our game — kept as reference because most BIOGLOW material online describes this edition.

Sources: raw/bioglow/fll-challenge-bioglow-rgr.pdf (official FIRST Robot Game Rulebook), firstinspires.blob.core.windows.net/fll/challenge/2026-27/fll-challenge-bioglow-season-overview.pdf

Last updated: 2026-08-08

Reference only. BOT Builders competes in Future Edition — see bioglow missions for our five missions and rules. Founders Edition is the SPIKE-based classic robot game: one team, one robot, 15 missions, 13 mission models. Its rules, hardware, and scoring do not transfer.

Format

2.5-minute match, 15 biodiversity-inspired missions, one robot. Four equally weighted parts: Core Values, Innovation Project, Robot Design, Robot Game. Champion's Award comes from all four. (source: bioglow-season-overview.pdf)

Mission Detail

M01 Drone Survey — Drone no longer touching the mat: 20. Bonus: and the LiDAR map completely flipped over with the scan marker at least partly in the survey area: +10.

M02 Exploding Seeds — Seeds no longer touching the stalk: 10 each.

M03 Flip the Rock — Research flag is down: 20. Bonus: and the rock returned to its original starting position: +10.

M04 Lucky Leaves — One leaf completely removed, no longer touching the nest: 10. Bonus: and the second leaf completely removed with the katydid in its original starting position: +20. If the katydid is completely removed from the nest at any time the bonus is lost; if it ends the match outside the leaf habitat even partly, the mission scores zero.

M05 Reaching Roots — Plant root partially extended: 10, OR completely extended: 20. Only one condition possible.

M06 Leafcutter Frenzy — Ant touching the nest and leaf fragments contained within it: 10 per fragment.

M07 Humongous Fungus — Mycelium completely extended: 20. Bonus: connection formed between one team's extended mycelium and the opposing team's fully extended plant root, two bonuses possible: +10 each. They need not touch. Unavailable in remote competition or with no opposing team.

M08 Tangled — The vine is touching the mat: 30.

M09 Research Platform — Research platform raised: 10. Camera trap deployed: 10. Seed no longer touching the tree: 10.

M10 Fragile Microhabitats — Spider habitat in its original starting position: 10. Snail habitat in its original starting position: 10.

M11 Window to the Past — Root cover down, touching the mat: 20.

M12 Forest Elder — Cane completely raised, touching the tree: 20. Support tie around the post: 10.

M13 Keystone Species — Team's keystone species on the restoration platform and young trees raised: 30. Built from bag 20; counts as equipment and is inspected pre-match.

M14 Seeds of Renewal — Seeds contained within the replantation station: 5 each. Bonus: and touching the mat: +5 each.

M15 Biocentric Architecture — Nesting canopy raised: 10. Garden skylight completely in: 10. Compost hatch completely opened, touching the mat: 10. Environmental bonus +10 for completing the greatest ecological need for the dock the model sits on — mine dock → nesting canopy, city dock → garden skylight, farm dock → compost hatch. Only one bonus possible.

Non-Mission Points

Equipment Inspection — 20. Robot and all equipment fit completely in one launch area, under 12 in. (305 mm).

Precision Tokens — up to 50. Six tokens to start. Interrupting the robot outside home costs one.

Tokens left 6 5 4 3 2 1
Points 50 50 35 25 15 10

No Equipment Constraint symbol — where shown, "a mission model cannot earn points if it is touching equipment at the end of the match," applying only to that mission.

Gracious Professionalism — referees score 2–4 points per match, added to the Core Values score. ACCOMPLISHED (3) is the common result. A team that cannot run its robot can still earn GP points; a no-show earns none. See gracious professionalism.
