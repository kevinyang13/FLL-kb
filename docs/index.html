Hardware Alert

ACTION

Short one single motor and one controller

The Future Edition rulebook sets equipment by type and quantity, not kit. Rule 5 requires 2× color sensor, 2× double motor, 2× controller, 2× single motor.

Our Grades 6–8 kit (#45522) supplies one of each. Even counting the earlier 3–5 kit (#45521), which has no single motor and no controller at all, the team is still one single motor and one controller short of a legal match setup. Two #45522 kits would meet it exactly.

This also closes the earlier question about whether the 3–5 division allows the 6–8 kit: it does not merely allow it — it requires that hardware. Source one more single motor and controller before the Oct 25 design freeze. See Missions & hardware rules.
