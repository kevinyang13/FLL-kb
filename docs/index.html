Event Day

2 tracks

Two things happen at a tournament, scored separately. The order between them is set by your local organizer — the judging slot may fall before, between, or after your matches.

Judging Session — 24 min, one room
  1. 2WelcomeJudges introduce themselves while the team sets up
  2. 5Project PresentationThe team presents their project work
  3. 3Project Q&AJudges ask clarifying questions
  4. 5Engineering Design PresentationDesign work and game strategy
  5. 3Engineering Design Q&AJudges ask clarifying questions
  6. 3Core Values Q&AQuestions about Core Values
  7. 3FeedbackRecognition and recommendations
  8. 10Judges deliberateTeam has left; rubric completed before the next team
Each Match
  1. —Setup for inspection
  2. —Pre-match inspectionFits its area, under 8 in.; field hardware handed over
  3. —Setup for matchSlot and pair hardware, assign one player per role
  4. 2:30MatchStarts on a countdown; all players stop at time
  5. —ScoringReferee reviews with the team; agreement makes it official
  6. —Remove equipment & reset

At least 3 matches. Rank is the average across all of them — not the best. One bad match cannot be dropped.

Three rubric rows teams miss: exploring multiple design options for each role tool; explaining cooperative strategy with the other team; and showing that all members contributed — that one appears on both rubrics. Full rubrics and all 10 awards: judging & awards.
