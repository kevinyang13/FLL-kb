# Weekly Documentation TODO

**Summary**: What gets written down each session, which document it goes in, who captures it, and which scored rubric row it feeds.

**Sources**: raw/bioglow/fll-future-3-8-judging-rubric.pdf, fll-future-3-8-judging-flowchart.pdf

**Last updated**: 2026-08-08

---

## Why This Exists

Judging is **one 24-minute session** with no retry. Everything the judges hear has to have been captured weeks earlier, and three of the four scored areas are decided in that room.

Every item below maps to a real rubric row. If a capture has no rubric reason it is not on this list.

**Kids capture. Adults make sure it happened.** The notebook lane never writes content — see [[team-roster]].

---

> **Printable version:** [Weekly Documentation Checklist](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OiZPpMt1udZAydX1XcIgwj3w82iCB9gEn6XSfS3NYY4/edit) — one per session, in `08 Templates/`.

> **Where each record lives:** everything on this page goes in the **kids' Drive and notebook** — that is what judges read, and the students write it. The wiki's [[season-journal]] is the separate high-level story of the season, kept by the coaches.

## The Last 10 Minutes

Stop building 10 minutes early. Every session, without exception:

| # | Capture | Where | Who |
|--:|---------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Weekly notebook entry — goal, what we did, what worked, what didn't, **decisions and why**, next steps | `01 Engineering Notebook/Weekly Entries/` | **Notebook Lead** (rotates) |
| 2 | Photos of anything built or changed today | `07 Media/` | **Photographer** (rotates) |
| 3 | Each student's own tool log — version, what changed, what failed | `02 Robot Game/Tool Designs/<role>/` | **Each student** |
| 4 | Any source used today | `04 Innovation Project/Sources Log/` | **Whoever used it** |
| 5 | Export every `.lecp` file touched today | `03 Software/LECP Projects/` | **Specialist** |
| 6 | Who did what today | Notebook entry | **Notebook Lead** |

Item 5 is the one that bites: Coding Canvas saves to the device, not the cloud. See [[google-drive]].

## By Scored Area

### Core Values — 25% of the Champion's score

No rubric of its own; it is harvested from rows inside the other two rubrics plus a Gracious Professionalism score referees record at **every match**. It accumulates all season and cannot be crammed.

| Capture | Where | Who | Feeds |
|---------|-------|-----|-------|
| Contribution note per student, every session | Notebook entry | Notebook Lead | *"All members meaningfully contribute"* — appears on **both** rubrics |
| Core Values activity done today | `06 Core Values/` | Any student | Core Values Q&A |
| GP moments — helped another team, shared parts, handled a setback well | `06 Core Values/` | Any student | GP score, Core Values Award |
| Photos showing the whole team working | `07 Media/` | Photographer | Contribution evidence |

**Watch for:** with five students and four match roles, one child rotates out each match. Record what they did anyway — judges ask about every member.

### Project

| Capture | Where | Who | Feeds rubric row |
|---------|-------|-----|------------------|
| Project Path research notes | `04 …/Project Paths/<role>/` | **Each student, own path** | *"Explains how the guided research influenced their project direction"* |
| Every source, logged when used | `04 …/Sources Log/` | Whoever used it | *"Uses multiple relevant sources"* |
| Ideas considered **and rejected**, with reasons | Innovation Project workbook | Whole team | *"Explored multiple ideas and clearly explains reasoning for selection"* |
| Expert interview notes + **what we changed because of it** | `04 …/Expert Interviews/` | Interviewing students | *"Iterative process… based on research or feedback"* |
| Prototype photos at each version | `04 …/Prototype/` | Photographer | *"Clear visuals, models, or prototypes"* |
| Users, impact, feasibility, trade-offs | Workbook, Part 6 | Whole team | The three **Implement** rows |
| User/expert feedback and the change it caused | `04 …/Feedback/` | Whole team | *"Iterative process"* |

See [[project-paths]] and [[innovation-project]].

### Engineering Design

| Capture | Where | Who | Feeds rubric row |
|---------|-------|-----|------------------|
| Tool version log — sketch or photo, what changed | `02 …/Tool Designs/<role>/` | Each student, own tool | *"Custom mechanical builds"* |
| **Rejected designs, photographed before dismantling** | `02 …/Tool Designs/Explored and Rejected/` | Each student | *"Explores multiple design options for **each role tool** before selecting one to test"* |
| Testing tallies — 10 runs, successes, percent | `02 …/Testing Logs/` | Technician + tester | *"Follows a clear testing process and documents results"* |
| What changed **because of** a test result | Notebook + testing log | Whoever changed it | *"Uses testing results to improve designs"* |
| Code notes — what each program does, who wrote it | `03 Software/Code Notes/` | Specialist | *"Custom coding that supports their mission strategy"* |
| Mission strategy changes and why | Notebook, Mission Strategy | Whole team | *"Clear mission strategy that guides design decisions"* |
| How the four roles hand off, and the plan for the other team | Notebook | Whole team | *"Cooperative game strategy between player roles and with another team"* |

The last row is the one teams forget. See [[judging-and-awards]].

**Photograph before dismantling.** A rebuilt tool erases its own evidence — the photo is all that survives.

## Judging Session Prep

The session runs welcome 2 · project 5 · project Q&A 3 · engineering design 5 · design Q&A 3 · Core Values Q&A 3 · feedback 3. Full detail: [[judging-and-awards]].

| Prep item | Where | Who | By |
|-----------|-------|-----|----|
| Project script with timings and speakers | `05 …/Project (5 min)/` | Whole team | Week 9 |
| Engineering Design script — must include **game strategy** | `05 …/Engineering Design (5 min)/` | Whole team | Week 10 |
| Every student has a speaking part | Both scripts | Notebook Lead checks | Week 10 |
| Q&A answers for the predictable questions | `05 …/QA Prep/` | Whole team | Week 12 |
| Notebook and testing log packed to bring into the room | — | Notebook lane adult | Week 15 |

Bring the notebook and testing log **into the judging room**. Pointing at real data beats remembering.
