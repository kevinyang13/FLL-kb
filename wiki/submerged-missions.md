# SUBMERGED Missions (2024-25)

**Summary**: All 15 robot game missions for the FLL 2024-25 SUBMERGED season, with scoring conditions, point values, and rookie notes.

**Sources**: komurobo.com/fll/2024-submerged, firstinspires.blob.core.windows.net (official rulebook), fllcasts.com

**Last updated**: 2026-05-12

---

## Season Overview

Theme: ocean exploration. 4ft × 8ft mat with underwater world — coral, sharks, whales, shipwrecks, research vessels. 15 missions + precision tokens. 2.5 min per run. Top score of 3 rounds counts.

**Rookies expected to complete 2–3 missions reliably.** See [[robot-game]] for match format and testing protocol.

---

## Precision Tokens

Start each match with 6 tokens (max **50 pts**). Lose one token each time robot is touched outside home base. Final token score:

| Tokens remaining | Points |
|-----------------|--------|
| 6 or 5 | 50 |
| 4 | 35 |
| 3 | 25 |
| 2 | 15 |
| 1 | 10 |
| 0 | 0 |

Strategy: avoid needing to be touched. Every touch costs points.

## M01 — Coral Nursery

**Max: 50 pts** | Location: near home base | **Good for rookies**

Secure coral structures in the nursery until strong enough for the reef.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Coral tree hanging on its support | 20 |
| + coral tree bottom in its holder | +10 |
| Coral buds flipped up | 20 |

Notes:
- Near home = short travel, less drift error
- Coral buds (20 pts) just require pushing a lever — no grabber needed
- Partial credit: easy to get 20 pts, harder to get full 50

---

## M02 — Shark

**Max: 30 pts**

Move the shark out of its cave and into the habitat.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Shark no longer touching cave | 20 |
| Shark touching mat and partly in habitat | 10 |

Notes: Two-part mission. Pushing shark out of cave gets 20 pts alone. Getting it into habitat requires more precision.

---

## M03 — Coral Reef

**Max: 20+ pts** (depends on reef segments placed)

Flip the coral reef up and return reef segments to the mat.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Coral reef flipped up, not touching mat | 20 |
| Each reef segment standing upright outside home | 5 each |

Notes: Reef segments are transported from home area. Each segment placed = 5 pts. Combined with M01 for efficient runs (both near home side).

---

## M04 — Scuba Diver

**Max: 40 pts**

Transport the scuba diver from the coral nursery to the coral reef support.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Diver no longer touching nursery | 20 |
| Diver hanging on reef support | 20 |

Notes: Diver has a loop — robot needs a hook or passive attachment to carry and hang it. Often combined with M01/M03 run since models are near each other.

---

## M05 — Angler Fish

**Max: 30 pts**

Latch the anglerfish inside the shipwreck.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Angler fish latched in shipwreck | 30 |

Notes: All-or-nothing. Requires pushing anglerfish into the shipwreck cavity until it latches. Precise alignment needed.

---

## M06 — Raise the Mast

**Max: 30 pts**

Raise the shipwreck's mast until the latch locks it in place.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Mast completely raised (latch prevents return) | 30 |

Notes: Often combined with M07. Robot lifts the back of the ship. Lever mechanism — all-or-nothing scoring.

---

## M07 — Kraken's Treasure

**Max: 20 pts**

Retrieve the treasure chest from the kraken's nest.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Treasure chest completely outside kraken's nest | 20 |

Notes: Kraken tentacles close if ship is lifted too high (during M06). Combined M06+M07 is a popular 50-pt run. Chest is also needed for M15.

---

## M08 — Artificial Habitat

**Max: 40 pts** (4 segments × 10 pts)

Stack artificial habitat segments upright.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Each segment completely flat and upright | 10 each |

Notes: 4 segments total. No Equipment Constraint — robot can touch model during run. Partial credit: each segment scored independently.

---

## M09 — Unexpected Encounter

**Max: 30 pts**

Release the unknown creature and get it into the cold seep.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Creature released | 20 |
| Creature partly in cold seep | 10 |

Notes: Releasing (20 pts) can be done by pushing a platform. Getting creature into cold seep (10 pts) requires either catching or guiding it.

---

## M10 — Send Over the Submersible

**Max: 40 pts**

Launch the submersible from your side to the opposing side of the field.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Yellow flag down (submersible launched) | 30 |
| Submersible closer to opposing field | 10 |

Notes: Robot flips a lever to launch. Quick to execute if approach is reliable. Bonus 10 pts requires submersible to travel far enough.

---

## M11 — Sonar Discovery

**Max: 30 pts**

Use sonar to reveal whales hidden under the ocean floor.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| One whale revealed | 20 |
| Both whales revealed (bonus) | +10 |

Notes: Push/rotate the sonar disc to reveal whales. Revealing one is straightforward; both requires more rotation or a second pass.

---

## M12 — Feed the Whale

**Max: varies** (10 pts per krill piece)

Collect krill pieces and deposit them in the whale's mouth.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Each krill piece at least partly in whale's mouth | 10 each |

Notes: Multiple krill on the mat. Robot needs a collection mechanism (scoop/basket). High potential points if many krill collected in one run.

---

## M13 — Change Shipping Lanes

**Max: 20 pts**

Move the cargo ship into the new shipping lane.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Ship in new lane, touching mat | 20 |

Notes: Push mission — robot nudges the ship along the mat into the correct lane. Simple but requires accurate positioning to avoid pushing it past the lane.

---

## M14 — Sample Collection

**Max: 55 pts** (not including bonus)

Collect various ocean samples and artifacts.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Water sample outside its area | 5 |
| Seabed sample not touching seabed | 10 |
| Plankton sample not touching kelp | 10 |
| Trident piece not touching shipwreck | 20 |
| Bonus: both trident pieces separated | +10 |

Notes: Many independent items — high partial credit. Samples also count toward M15 if delivered to research vessel.

---

## M15 — Research Vessel

**Max: 45 pts** (varies by items delivered)

Deliver collected items to the research vessel and dock at port.

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Each sample in cargo area | 5 each |
| Each trident part in cargo area | 5 each |
| Treasure chest in cargo area | 5 |
| Port latch in vessel loop (docking) | 20 |

Notes: High-reward end-of-run delivery mission. Items from M14 (samples), M07 (treasure chest), and the trident chain into M15. Docking (20 pts) requires precise alignment with the port.

## Rookie Strategy

Best starting missions based on proximity, simplicity, and partial-credit potential:

1. **M01 Coral Nursery** — near home, lever mechanism, 20 pts just for buds
2. **M13 Change Shipping Lanes** — simple push, 20 pts, reliable
3. **M11 Sonar Discovery** — rotate disc, 20 pts for one whale
4. **M06 + M07** — combined 50-pt run once approach is dialed in

See [[robot-design-principles]] for reliability-first strategy and 10-run testing protocol.
