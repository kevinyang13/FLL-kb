## Team Agreements

**For parents:**
- Text group in advance if child will miss a session (pair/group work is disrupted by absences)
- "Embrace the Mess" — step back, let kids figure it out

**For kids (Team Spirit):**
1. Share the fun and the work — robot/computers belong to the whole team
2. Listen to understand — "Tell me more about that idea" not "That won't work"
3. Respect space and teammates — kind words, talk about the idea not the person
4. Celebrate mistakes — ask "What did we learn?" and try a new way

[[gracious-professionalism]] is the heart of the program: "how we work together is far more important than winning." (source: FLL_Rookie_Team_Charter_Registration_Ready.pdf)

## Registration

Form requires: student legal name, preferred nickname, date of birth, parent/guardian contacts (up to 2). Signed by student and parent. Sent to coaches for official FIRST registration.
