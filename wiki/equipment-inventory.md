## Against the Rules

Rule 5 specifies equipment by **type and quantity**, not by kit, and allows exactly:

| Hardware | Rule 5 | We have | Spare |
|----------|-------:|--------:|------:|
| Color sensor | 2 | **3** | +1 |
| Double motor | 2 | **3** | +1 |
| Single motor | 2 | **3** | +1 |
| Controller | 2 | **3** | +1 |
| Connection cards | 4 max (1 required + 3 optional) | **6** | +2 |

**Requirement met, with one spare of each device.**

Two of each is a **maximum**, not just a minimum — three kits does not mean more hardware on the field.

The 3-5 kit contains no single motor and no controller at all, so it could not have fielded a legal team on its own. See [[kit-comparison-3-5-vs-6-8]].

## Match-Day Allocation

Before inspection the team hands over hardware to operate the **field models**, fully charged and with no attachments. That hardware counts toward the same 2-per-type limit. (source: fll-future-3-8-bioglow-rulebook.pdf rule 8)

| Device | #1 goes to | #2 goes to |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Double motor | **Grand tree** (field) | Driving base — Driver |
| Single motor | **Hive** (field) | Robotic tool — Technician |
| Color sensor | **Young forest** (field) | Robotic tool — Technician |
| Controller | Driver | Specialist |
| Connection card | **Referee** holds it during the match | Team use, up to 3 more |

So the robot side effectively runs on **one** double motor, **one** single motor, and **one** color sensor. Design for that. See [[robot-design-principles]].

The color sensor in the young forest pairs with the single motor in the hive on the **opposite** field via the referee-held card — that link is what makes M01's cross-field bonus possible. See [[bioglow-missions]].

## The Spare Is Thinner Than It Looks

Two of each device are committed every match — one to the field, one to the robot. The third is the **only** fallback for a flat battery or a dead motor.

Because the kits are family-owned and live in three different houses, that spare only exists if all three families actually turn up with theirs.

**Two habits worth keeping:**
- **All three kits charged before every event**, not just the two being fielded.
- **Confirm all three are coming** to tournaments and to the Week 9 first timed run.
