## What was said — part 1

*A speaker from the program delivery team; the transcript does not name who.*

### Date and place

- A **Sunday** date is possible as well as a Saturday. The speaker believes the last weekend of January is **the 30th or the 31st** and asked coaches to **keep both days open**. (30 Jan 2027 is a Saturday and 31 Jan a Sunday.)
- **Two locations are expected for the region: East Los Angeles and San Diego.** Neither venue is fixed; the team is working to lock them in.

### They are asking teams for kit

- **LEGO has not given the region anything to run the events this year**, so teams are asked to **lend part of their kit** to run the robot-game matches.
- Interested teams should email **all three** of Paul Kass, Cathy Schulz and Sarah Rodriguez; they will coordinate.

### Event-day schedule

| Time | What |
|------|------|
| **7:30 am** | Check-in |
| **~8:00 am** | Practice matches |
| **8:30 am** | Judging starts cycling through |
| **Ends by 5:00 pm** | Expected closer to **3:00 pm**, but they plan for more time than needed |

Teams should **plan to stay the whole day**. The speaker understood some children have earlier commitments and asked that they stay if they can. The format is described as close to Founders Edition for veteran teams.

### Compliance is being enforced this year

To register **and** compete, **both lead coaches** must be California Youth Protection Program compliant. On the FIRST dashboard that means:

1. **Live scan** (fingerprinting)
2. The **2-hour California mandated-reporter training** (links are on the dashboard)
3. The **DOJ form** that lets FIRST see live-scan results each year

- **Do not wait.** The live scan takes **2 to 3 weeks** to register through FIRST.
- Check that each item shows **green** on the dashboard.
- FIRST matches a coach's profile to their live-scan record using only **email, first name and last name**. Keep all three as consistent as possible. If a name or email is common, contact **safety at FIRST** so they can identify the record.

| 5 | Engineering design Q&A | 3 min |

The speaker then said "that's the completion." The 3-minute judging-feedback segment in [[judging-and-awards]] was not mentioned; whether it was left out or changed is not clear from this part.
