# The FIRST / LEGO Education Split — What It Means For Us

**Summary**: LEGO Education and FIRST are ending a partnership that began in 1998. This is **the last FIRST LEGO League season ever**. Here is who is doing what afterwards, and what it means for our kits and our team.

**Sources**: education.lego.com/en-us/first-lego-league/partnership-update/; community.firstinspires.org/first-lego-education-partnership-update; first-lego-league.org/en/transition-and-future

**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

---

## The Short Version

**LEGO Education chose not to renew.** After 28 years, the two organisations are separating.

> *"The 2026-2027 FIRST LEGO League season will be our last together."* — LEGO Education
>
> *"LEGO Education has chosen not to renew our partnership agreement. The 2026-2027 season will be our final season, after which FIRST LEGO League will conclude."* — FIRST

**This season — the one we are in — is the final FIRST LEGO League.** After it, the programme splits in two and each organisation goes its own way.

---

## Who Goes Where

| | **LEGO Education** | **FIRST** |
|---|---|---|
| **New programme** | **LEGO® League** — launches August 2027 | *"Next generation of K-8 program experiences"* — unnamed |
| **Hardware** | SPIKE, legacy systems **and** Computer Science & AI | **Not announced** |
| **Events** | Local, regional, national and international — same shape as now | Not announced |
| **Detail available** | Substantial | Very little |

**Yes, LEGO will run its own events.** That was one of the open questions — the transition page states LEGO League keeps *"local, regional, national, and international opportunities."* The competition structure survives; the name and the partner change.

**Whether FIRST builds new hardware is genuinely unknown.** FIRST says only that it is *"actively developing our own next generation of K-8 program experiences"* and will *"share additional information in the coming months."* No platform, no name, no dates. That is not us failing to find it — it has not been announced.

## The Timeline

| Season | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **2026-2027** *(now)* | **The final FIRST LEGO League.** Runs exactly as planned, both editions — Founders on SPIKE, Future on CS & AI. Nothing about our season changes. |
| **2027-2028** | Becomes **LEGO League**. Both hardware families supported: SPIKE, legacy systems, and CS & AI. Events continue at every level. FIRST separately launches something for K-8, as yet undescribed. |
| **2028-2029** | Single edition. **CS & AI becomes the primary robotics platform.** |

## What This Means For Us — The Practical Answer

### Our hardware is the right hardware

The team owns **three Computer Science & AI kits (#45522)** and competes in **Future Edition**. Read the timeline again with that in mind:

- **2027-28** — CS & AI is fully supported in LEGO League
- **2028-29** — CS & AI becomes **the primary platform**

**We are already on the platform everything is moving toward.** The kits are not a one-season purchase; they are the thing the next several seasons are built on. See [[equipment-inventory]].

### The teams with a real question are the SPIKE ones

Founders Edition runs on SPIKE. That edition ends, and by 2028-29 SPIKE is no longer the primary platform. A team that invested in SPIKE this year faces a migration we do not.

This also explains something that looked odd earlier in the season: why LEGO introduced Future Edition *now*, mid-partnership. It was building the runway for its own programme. Choosing Future Edition turns out to have been the forward-looking choice, whether or not it was picked for that reason.

### Nothing about this season changes

Every rule, mission, rubric and award on this wiki still applies. The split affects **2027-28 onward**, not the season we are in. Do not let it distract from the Oct 25 design freeze or the Nov 15 tournament.

## Questions Worth Asking, With Honest Answers

**"Is FIRST LEGO League going away?"**
The *name* is. The activity is not. LEGO League continues it with the same competition structure; FIRST will run something separate for the same age group.

**"Will our team have somewhere to compete next year?"**
Almost certainly two somewheres — LEGO League, and whatever FIRST announces. The awkward part is that the community may split between them, so local event availability could vary by region.

**"Do we need to buy new kits?"**
No. CS & AI is where LEGO League is heading.

**"Is FIRST making new hardware?"**
Unknown. Not announced either way.

**"Which one should we join in 2027-28?"**
Too early. LEGO League has published a plan; FIRST has published an intention. Revisit when FIRST announces details — likely well before August 2027.

## A Note For The Kids

Worth telling them plainly, because they will hear rumours: **nothing about this season changes.** The missions are the missions, the tournament is still in November, and the robot they are building is the robot they will compete with.

What is changing is that next year the competition gets a new name. The team, the kits and the skills all carry over.
