## What it adds to the season journal

[[season-journal]] mixes robot and project. The timeline keeps only the Innovation Project, and adds what the team chat holds that the journal did not:

- **The 27 September written feedback from R!SE H!GH** — praise, and a next-steps list that includes *test tubes with real host plants*, *find two or more experts*, *estimate the cost* and *memorise the lines instead of reading off the board*. The journal's gap "what the mentors actually said" is closed for this date; for **1 November it is still open**.
- **The 15 October five-kids script** — where [[lacewing-play]] began — and the rubric reading that gave the team its keywords: *Evidence, Iteration, Consistency, Teamwork*.
- **The 2 November Performance Guide** — five numbered sections, one per child, matching the board; speaking order Cheryl, Kei, Lola, Lindsey, Kyle; *"Your board helps you remember."* That is the "board as memory aid, play kept" option in practice, not a board replacing the play.
- **The second expert** — Cassandra Fieldson (science communicator, malaria researcher, ecologist), Zoom **Sunday 15 November, 1 pm**.
- **Feeding experiments** (5–7 October) and the first cocoon (14 October), with photos.
